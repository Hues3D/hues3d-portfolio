UpdateDevlog #005

Dressed for the Wasteland

Survivors can now wear what they find, bases get two tougher doors, and almost every screen in the game has been rebuilt.

Hues Studios · November 3, 2026 · 3 min read

The last two months were one of the biggest stretches of work on Remnants so far. Survivors can now wear what they find, bases get two tougher doors, and almost every screen in the game has been rebuilt, from the first loading frame to the hotbar. Here's what's new.

Clothing comes to Remnants

The headline feature is a full clothing system. Every piece you wear shows on your character, on your own arms and legs in first person, and on your sleeper after you log out, so what you see on another survivor is really what they're wearing.

Clothing follows layering rules. Each body region has an under layer and an over layer, and putting on something that clashes with what you're wearing takes the old piece off for you. No menus to fight with.

The first wardrobe includes:

  • Baseball Cap, Hoodie, Utility Pants and Military Boots, all hand-modelled and textured
  • Pork Pie Hat, T-Shirt and Shorts
  • The Rad Suit: a 46-piece full-body suit, gloves included, built for the parts of the map that don't want you there

Every clothing item also has its own model when dropped: a folded bundle on the ground instead of a generic loot bag. The drops share textures with the worn pieces, which sets things up for skins later.

Metal and Steel doors

Wooden doors now have two stronger options. The Metal Door and Steel Door each have their own model and health tier. The Steel Door adds a sliding viewing hatch you can open and close from the interact wheel, so you can check who's knocking first.

Locks have been refitted too. Key locks and code locks sit on the plate just above the handle on both faces of every door. Door damage has been rebalanced: doors no longer have a weak "soft side", and each tool now does a fixed amount of damage depending on the door's tier.

Building and world fixes

  • The hammer's upgrade wheel now shows material icons and costs instead of text, and steel is now its own resource
  • Closed an exploit where hitting a wall's corner from the protected side counted as soft-side damage
  • Foundations placed at an angle can no longer wedge into each other
  • Structure debris and rock fragments clear away faster, and fragments shrink as they fall
  • Chests, furnaces and the Settlement Registry are resized to match the world's scale. They now fit properly against your walls and stop crowding your base

Movement

Crouching now has real animation: a crouch idle and a motion-captured crouch walk that blend with your speed and keep your feet planted. It looks right from both first and third person.

A new face for the game

Based on direct feedback from our playtest community, the interface has been reworked from top to bottom:

  • A new loading screen with the Hues Studios ident, the Remnants title card, and a progress bar that tracks actual loading instead of a timer
  • A cleaner hotbar: a burnt-orange highlight on the selected slot and soft shadows behind icons
  • New vitals: health, hunger and thirst are now compact round badges whose icons fill up as the stat rises
  • A full Settings menu with rebindable keys, gameplay, graphics, audio and interface options. Your choices are saved to your account
  • Better scaling across PC screen sizes and aspect ratios

Fresh starts

New survivors now spawn with just a Primitive Pick and a Firefly Jar. Everything else has to be found or earned.

What's next

Clothing isn't finished yet. Full cloth and leather sets and three types of armour are in production. After that, the focus moves to combat and player damage, followed by persistent saving.

Thanks to everyone testing and sending feedback. Much of this update came from you. See you in the Remnants.

— Hues Studios
