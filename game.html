Remnants

A multiplayer survival and base-building game on Roblox. Gather from a hostile world, build a base that has to stand on its own, and hold onto what you've got, all with a hand-textured, line-driven look. Designed, modeled, and programmed by one developer.

Pillars

What the game is built around

01

Build it to last

Structural building where every piece needs support, five material tiers from thatch to steel, and upkeep that keeps a base standing only as long as you look after it.

02

Everything is earned

Resources come out of the world by hand. Nodes crumble as you work them, accurate hits pay off, and raw ore has to be smelted before it's worth anything.

03

A look of its own

Line-stylized 3D: hand-textured assets with drawn line detail, shaped to read clearly at gameplay distance first, with surface detail second.

In the game today

What's already built

Working, playable systems in the current playtest build.

Harvesting

Trees and ore nodes that break down in stages, sweet spots that reward accurate swings, and tools that each gather differently.

Building

Foundations, walls, floors, stairs and roofs that snap together, with raised and lowered foundations for uneven ground.

Stability & tiers

Pieces need real support or they collapse. Upgrade from thatch to wood, stone, metal and steel, each with a weak and strong side.

Claims & upkeep

A Settlement Registry claims your land. Keep it stocked or your base slowly decays back into the world.

Doors & locks

Doors with key locks or code locks, so only the people you trust get inside.

Storage & smelting

Chests and furnaces you place and pick back up. Furnaces turn ore into ingots, with charcoal left over.

Survival

Hunger and thirst that drain as you move, plus food, water sources, foraging and medicine.

Death & sleepers

Die and you drop a bag of your gear. Log off and your character stays behind asleep, and anyone can loot it.

Teams & chat

Groups of up to eight players with their own team chat and nameplates.

Characters & clothing

A custom character with recolorable hair, and layered clothing: caps, hoodies, utility pants and boots.

Feel

Sprint, crouch and free look, a first-person view with your own body, and per-item animations.

Impact & light

Hit effects driven by the material you strike, and a jar of live fireflies to light the way.

Roadmap

What comes next

Each phase should be playable end to end before the next one starts.

  1. Phase 1

    Crafting

    Recipes and workbenches, so a fresh player can go from bare hands to tools without any help.

  2. Phase 2

    Saving progress

    Bases, inventories, locks and upkeep timers that survive a server restart.

  3. Phase 3

    Combat

    Melee first, then bows, armor and eventually firearms.

  4. Phase 4

    Raiding

    Explosives and raid tools that put the building tiers to the test.

  5. Phase 5

    Loot & landmarks

    Scrap, loot crates, recyclers, and hand-built points of interest worth fighting over.

  6. Phase 6

    Progression

    Research and a tech tree, so a new player and a veteran want different things.

  7. Phase 7

    The world

    A real map with biomes, a day/night cycle, temperature and wildlife.

  8. Phase 8

    Launch

    Respawn points, onboarding, performance, audio and settings, then public servers.

Art direction

Shape first, texture second

The form of each asset does the first half of the work and the texture carries the second half. Wood grain follows the shape of each board, stone gets broad, hand-painted breakup, and drawn lines tie it all together.

Every asset goes through the same three stages, all documented in the devlog: a clean wireframe, a sculpt pass, and a final textured render.
