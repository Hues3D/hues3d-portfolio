Survive. Gather. Build.

A stylized survival and base-building game with a hand-textured, line-driven look, designed, modeled, and programmed by one developer.

Pillars

What the game is built around

01

A look of its own

Line-stylized 3D: hand-textured assets with drawn line detail, so the world feels illustrated rather than like another generic survival game.

02

Readable at a glance

Every tool, prop, and building piece is shaped to read clearly at gameplay distance first, with surface detail second.

03

Build your base

Modular building pieces with clean connection points, starting from rough twig foundations and growing from there.

Art direction

Shape first, texture second

The form of each asset does the first half of the work and the texture carries the second half. Wood grain follows the shape of each board, stone gets broad, hand-painted breakup, and drawn lines tie it all together.

Every asset goes through the same three stages, all documented in the devlog: a clean wireframe, a sculpt pass, and a final textured render.
