About

The developer

I'm James Hughes, known online as Hues: a 3D artist and game developer from Barbados, and the one person behind Hues Studios.

I started making 3D content at 13, and over the past seven years I've worked across nearly every stage of game development: hard-surface modeling, sculpting, UVs, baking, texturing, optimization, engine implementation, programming, animation, and game design.

Along the way I've contributed art to several Viking Studios projects, including Lost, Dust Wasteland Survival, and Project Delta, which taught me how to make production-ready assets inside an established pipeline.

My main focus is environment and prop art, especially texturing and material work: how shape, surface detail, and texture combine to give a game world its identity.

I hold an Associate Degree in Computer Science and I'm finishing my Bachelor's. That technical side is a big part of why I can take on a game solo. I understand the optimization and real-time limits the art has to live within, and I can write the systems myself.

The game documented on this site is my largest project yet, built start to finish on my own. The devlog is where I share how it's going.
